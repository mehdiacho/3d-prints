KEYED NOTES
1THE CABLE HAS ALREADY TORN AT THE LIGHT RING. The strain relief has pulled out of the head. The brace has to carry the cable past the tear, not only hold the head.
2THE VENT ENDS 8 FROM THE COLLAR. The plug body comes within 6.5 of it. Nothing may cover it, so any grip goes above, below or towards the screw (17.4 away).
3THE PLUG LEAVES STRAIGHT OUT OF THE REAR. The cable is forced over right at the head, which is what tore it. The brace should turn it through a set radius instead.
4ONLY THE SLEEVE HOLDS THE PLUG. About 3 of the sleeve stays outside and the collar (8.1) is smaller than the body (11.2), so the body never seats on the laptop. The brace has to fill that gap.
5THE REAR BAR HAS A TROUGH BEHIND IT. The bar is flat for 18.4, then the surface dips to 10 deep where the lid hinged. A clip over the bar can hook into that dip. A printed head is a later print there.
