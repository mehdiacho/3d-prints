KEYED NOTES
1THE CABLE HAS ALREADY TORN AT THE LIGHT RING. The strain relief has pulled out of the head and the cores are bare. The brace has to carry the cable past the tear, not only hold the head.
2THE VENT STARTS RIGHT BESIDE THE JACK. Nothing may cover it. Any grip on the laptop goes above, below or towards the screw.
3THE PLUG LEAVES STRAIGHT OUT OF THE REAR. Against the wall the cable is forced over at the head. The brace should turn it through a set radius instead.
4THE LAPTOP HAS NO LID. The hinge pockets are open. A printed head is a later print on this same rear edge; the brace stays clear of it.
