KEYED NOTES · SHEET 01
1FRONT AND BACK ARE NOT MIRROR IMAGES. The logo-face edge drops to S in the middle; the rivet-face edge rises to D. Both views are needed.
2THE BASE IS HIDDEN FROM THE FRONT. The logo-face skirt covers it. Tab, base and skirt are one piece with no gap between them.
3THE HOOK POINTS AT THE BLADE. It sets which wall of the socket carries the catch.
4THE TAB AND THE FOLDED BLADE SIT NEARER THE LOGO FACE. Tab 7.95 and blade 8.03 from the rivet face, in a head 15.80 thick. The buttons go on the logo face too.
5THESE AXES ARE LEFT-HANDED. x from the blade side, y from the rivet face, z down from the dome tip. A model built straight from them comes out mirrored, as body v3 did: mirror one axis once.
6SOME OF THE HEAD IS DRAWN APPROXIMATE. The rivet position (B7), the dome curve and the blade profile. None of them is used by the body.
