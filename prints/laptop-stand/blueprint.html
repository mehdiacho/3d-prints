KEYED NOTES
1THE LAPTOP RUNS WHILE IT STANDS. It drives an external screen, so the rear vent, the top vents and the intake underneath must all stay open. That rules the layout more than strength does.
2ONE HOLDER DOES BOTH JOBS, THE SAME WAY UP. The slot opens upward. A jaw on one side takes the desk's edge; on the desk, that jaw's lower arm is simply a foot.
3TWO SHORT HOLDERS, NOT ONE LONG ONE. The edge is 356 or 273 long and the printer's bed is 220. Two identical 60 long pieces, one near each end.
4A TALL THIN THING TIPS EASILY. On the desk about 7 N on the top edge knocks option A over. Hooked on the desk's edge it cannot tip. A wider foot would raise the 7 N.
5THE EDGE IN THE SLOT IS PROBABLY THINNER THAN 23. 23 is the rear bar. Forward of it the deck sits 6.6 lower, so the front and sides are likely about 16.
6EACH HOLDER PRINTS AS ITS CROSS-SECTION. Lying on an end face: no supports, and the layers run round the jaws and walls, the direction they are loaded in.
